Meter a request in one call.

Copy a key, send an event from your server, and the count shows up on the customer's meter in seconds.

import Tollgate from "@tollgate/node";

const tollgate = new Tollgate(process.env.TOLLGATE_KEY);

// one event per billable request
await tollgate.usage.record({
  customer: "cus_halden",
  meter: "api_requests",
  quantity: 1,
  idempotencyKey: req.id,
});

Questions

Something else? Write to billing@tollgate.example and a person replies within a day.

How does Tollgate count usage?

Your server sends one event per billable action, alone or in a batch. Each event carries a key, so a request you retry is counted once.

Can I change a price in the middle of a month?

Yes. A new price applies from the moment you save it. That month's invoice shows both rates, each on its own line.

What do my customers see?

A usage page with their meter, their plan and every past invoice. You can put it on your own domain, in your own colours.

How do my customers pay?

By card or bank transfer, in 40 currencies. Money lands in your bank account two days after a payment clears.

What happens when a payment fails?

Tollgate retries on a schedule you set and emails the customer a link to update their card. You decide if and when their keys pause.

Can I move from another billing tool?

Yes. Import customers, plans and open invoices from a CSV, then run both side by side for a month before you switch.
